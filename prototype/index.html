What works on your TCL 65C6K

Catalog / Applications sidebar, apps grid, Inputs, Clock, launcher SettingsWorksBuilt into the launcher fork. Nothing outside the launcher involved.
Frosted sidebar and blurred backdropWorksYour TV runs Google TV 14, which supports blur. Settings › Sidebar blur turns it off if it feels slow.
Continue Watching row from NuvioCheck on TVNuvio publishes it to Android TV, with wide artwork, progress and a play link. It does not mark the rows as readable by other apps, so a 2-minute test on your TV confirms the launcher can see them.
Artwork on the cardsWorksNuvio gives web links for its pictures; the launcher fork downloads them. That is its only internet use.
Popular Movies and other catalogs, Focused catalog, Choose catalogsNeeds Nuvio changeNuvio shares only Continue Watching with other apps. Its catalogs come from your addons inside Nuvio, so Nuvio has to publish them.
Film details, source list, player and its controlsWorksThese are Nuvio’s own screens. The launcher opens a film with Nuvio’s existing nuvio://detail link, so you get the real thing, kept current by Nuvio.
Nuvio Settings and Nuvio account in the sidebarOpens Nuvio homeNuvio has no link to open its settings or account screen, so both open Nuvio and you go to Settings there. A small Nuvio change makes them open the right page.
Stream selection (skip the source list)WorksSet inside Nuvio: Settings › Playback › Stream selection.
Catalog and ApplicationsThe first two items in the sidebar. Catalog opens on Nuvio's rows; Applications shows your apps, six to a row. Home on the remote returns to Catalog; Left from the first poster or app opens the sidebar.
Focused catalogThe large poster row sits in your catalog list, second by default. Settings in the sidebar sets which catalog it is, where it sits (top, second, middle, bottom), which catalogs appear, and the sidebar blur.
Details, then sources, then playerOK on a poster opens the film's page laid out like Nuvio's: Play (Resume if started), Add to library, Mark as watched, director, synopsis, then Creator and Cast and More like this. Play opens the source list, as Nuvio does by default.
Skipping the source listSidebar › Nuvio Settings › Playback › Stream selection. Switch it to First stream and Play goes straight to "Finding stream source" and the player. Back from the player then returns to the film page, as in Nuvio.
PlayerNuvio's layout: clock and end time top right, title, seek bar with no knob, icon buttons (Play, Subtitles, Audio, Sources, More › Speed, Aspect, Switch engine, External player, Stream info). Controls hide after 3 s; hold Left or Right to scrub faster; pause for 5 s to see "You're watching".
